  OEssentials — Version 7.1
  Minecraft 1.21 | Paper / Folia
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Bug Fixes
---------
- Fixed startup warnings about commands already registered by other plugins
  (WorldGuard, GriefDefender, vanilla server). Commands such as /heal, /god,
  /ban, /kick, /tp, /gamemode, /sell, /msg, /tell, /enchant, /ping, /clear,
  /clone, /kill, /bc and more now properly override conflicting registrations
  and take full control of those labels on startup. They will no longer silently
  fall back to the vanilla or third-party equivalent.

Internal
--------
- Removed leftover source files from the old fr.elias.oreoEssentials package
  that were causing build failures after the 7.0 refactor to fr.elias.oessentials.
- Cleaned up duplicate CMI migration classes and AdminHomesGUI stubs.
