===============================================================
  OEssentials - Version 7.1.7
  Minecraft 1.21 | Paper / Folia
===============================================================

A big one. OEssentials has been rebuilt on the new MinecraftModulith
module system, cross-server trading now works (and cannot dupe), and a
two-server test network with bot players turned up and fixed around
thirty bugs across trading, the auction house, orders, player warps,
moderation and cross-server sync.


Cross-Server Trading (rebuilt)
------------------------------
- Players can now trade with players on another server of the network.
  Send /trade <player>; they accept with /trade <your name> on their
  server, and both trade menus open.
- Offers update live on both servers: an item placed or taken back shows
  up in the other player's menu within a fraction of a second.
- Built to be dupe-proof. Offered items are stored in one MongoDB trade
  record, and every step (offer, take back, confirm, complete, cancel)
  is a single all-or-nothing database update, so two servers can never
  both "win". Each player receives their items exactly once, on whatever
  server they are on.
- Changing your offer resets both confirmations, and a confirmation only
  counts for the offers the player actually saw.
- Leaving or switching servers mid-trade cancels it; your items come back
  a few seconds after you next join any server.
- If your partner's server goes down mid-trade, the trade is cancelled
  after 15 seconds and your items are returned straight away.
- All clicks in the trade menu are handled by the plugin, so drag,
  shift-click, number-key and double-click tricks cannot move items.
- Requires essentials.storage: mongodb, RabbitMQ and the trade
  cross-server setting (on by default). Without MongoDB storage, trading
  stays between players on the same server.
- The old cross-server trade system, which never worked and could lose or
  duplicate items, has been removed.

Crash Protection
----------------
- Trades are crash-safe: every item a trade moves is saved together with
  a receipt in the player's own data. After a server crash, the next join
  checks the receipts, so an item is never duplicated or lost (tested by
  force-killing servers mid-trade and right after a trade completed).
- Inventory sync used to save a player only when they left, so a crash
  lost everything since they joined. Online players are now also saved
  every 60 seconds (new option playersync.autosave-seconds; 0 = only on
  quit), so a crash loses at most about a minute.
- Fixed: a quick server switch could overwrite a player's real synced
  inventory with the stale copy the new server held before loading it.
  Nothing is saved for a player until their inventory has loaded.

Trading Fixes (same server)
---------------------------
- Fixed: after a cancelled trade, both trade menus kept re-opening in a
  loop until the next trade.
- Fixed: grey border panes from the trade menu could end up in players'
  inventories.
- Fixed: the trade could write copies of offered items into another
  inventory the other player had open (a possible dupe).
- Same-server trades now complete directly on the server, without going
  through RabbitMQ.

Player Warps
------------
- Extra warp slots from /pw addwarps now apply on every server of the
  network (they only worked on the server where they were given).
  Existing grants are imported automatically.
- /pw list <player> and /pw amount <player> work for players on other
  servers and for offline players (they used to say "not online").
- Every /pw command and /pwwhitelist was tested across two servers:
  teleports, costs (paid to the owner), locks, passwords, whitelists,
  managers, limits, menus, rename, reset, setowner, removeall and reload.

Auction House and Orders
------------------------
- Fixed: the auction house buy button did nothing (the menu replaced it
  with an inactive copy one tick after opening).
- Fixed: the auction house and player vaults never used MongoDB, even
  with essentials.storage: mongodb, so listings and vaults were not
  shared between servers.
- Fixed: orders did not sync across servers with MongoDB storage and
  cross-server orders enabled.
- Fixed: typing an order quantity or price also posted it to global chat
  on every server.
- Fixed: the auction house, orders and portals never wrote their default
  config files (the auction house created four empty files, and order
  prompts showed "Missing lang key"). Empty files left by older versions
  are refilled automatically; customised files are not touched.
- The bundled auction house gui.yml is now in English (it was French).

Cross-Server Network
--------------------
- Warnings are now shared across servers with MongoDB storage; existing
  warnings in data/warnings.yml are imported once.
- Fixed: a player who had just switched servers could look offline to
  other servers (cross-server /msg answered "Player not found", and the
  same affected trade, /tpa and /pw list). Two separate causes in the
  player directory are fixed.
- RabbitMQ addresses ending in "/" now work (they selected an empty
  vhost that RabbitMQ rejects), and connection errors show the real
  reason instead of "null".

Moderation and Commands
-----------------------
- Fixed: very large durations overflowed. /mute with a huge duration
  could become permanent, /ban crashed with an internal error, and jail,
  maintenance and warning durations could wrap around. Durations are now
  capped at 100 years, and /ban says when one is too long.
- Fixed: /head messages showed a literal {target}.
- commands-toggle.yml now also applies to commands that were registered
  before it loaded.
- Commands of optional modules (such as skins and player vaults) are only
  registered when their module is running.

Startup, Reload and Shutdown
----------------------------
- Fixed: OEssentials disabled itself at startup on servers without
  PlaceholderAPI.
- Fixed: /balance, /money and the shop failed on servers without Vault.
- Fixed: errors when stopping the server with players online ("Could not
  close nametag/nametagManager"); nametag entities are now removed
  properly on shutdown.
- Fixed: the jump pads data file was not created on first start.
- /oereload: each feature now reloads itself. Fixed the mob health bar
  listener being registered again on every reload.
- The public OEssentials API is now always registered (it was missing
  when the currency feature was disabled).
- Fixed an error when reading malformed /back locations.

Performance
-----------
- Tested with 25 players using chat, homes, money, messages, the auction
  house and orders continuously for 4 minutes: the server held 20 TPS
  with an average tick of 3-4 ms.

New and Changed Settings
------------------------
- playersync.autosave-seconds (config.yml, default 60): how often online
  players' synced inventories are saved. 0 saves only on quit.
- Cross-server trading uses the existing trade cross-server setting in
  settings.yml and needs essentials.storage: mongodb and RabbitMQ.

For Developers
--------------
- OEssentials is now made of 56 modules with enforced boundaries
  (MinecraftModulith 0.5.3): modules only use each other through small
  published contracts, never through internals.
- The public API (fr.elias.oessentials.api, used by other plugins) is
  unchanged, with one exception: OEssentials#getTradeBroker() is removed,
  as it only exposed the old cross-server trade system.
- Some types returned by OEssentials getters moved package; the full list
  is in src/test/resources/bootstrap/class-relocations.json.
- Modules now provide their own PlaceholderAPI placeholders and their own
  part of the public API.
- Network packets have fixed ids. Ids 27000-27005 and 27014 belonged to
  the old trade system and stay reserved; 27029 is new.

Upgrade Notes
-------------
- Remove the old OEssentials jar from the plugins folder so only 7.1.7
  loads.
- For cross-server trading, warnings, auction house, orders and player
  vault sharing, use essentials.storage: mongodb with RabbitMQ.
- MongoDB 4.2 or newer is required for the cross-server trade partner
  check.
